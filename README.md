# Hey, I'm Swarnim

By day I help build gaming things at **Alienware** — product strategy and market intelligence, which mostly means Excel and PowerPoint. By night I tinker with AI to scratch my own itches.

Not a developer. Not technical. I got into this after watching a friend get way too excited about a cooking app he'd built — didn't understand a word of it, installed Claude Code anyway, and started figuring it out by breaking things. Turns out the technical part was the excuse, not the obstacle.

I design and decide; the AI does the typing. Somehow that's been enough to ship a few real things.

## What I'm building

- **[parsaveables-v2](https://github.com/swarnimb/parsaveables-v2)** — disc golf tournament tracker for my friend group. AI reads the scorecards; there's an in-app economy called PULP, because why not.
- **[sbdev-org](https://github.com/swarnimb/sbdev-org)** — a lightweight agent framework so other non-technical people can build with Claude Code without losing the plot.
- **[gamerPCcoach](https://github.com/swarnimb/gamerPCcoach)** — preps and babysits your PC for a gaming session, then tells you what you did wrong afterward.

They range from "actually works" to "works on my machine." Currently cooking a personal finance aggregator and a credit-card-benefits tool (both mostly for me), with a multi-agent debate setup next, for decisions I'd otherwise overthink.
